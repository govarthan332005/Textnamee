## ⚠️ COMPLIANCE NOTES

- Daily-return investment products in India fall under SEBI / RBI scrutiny. Make sure your business model is legally vetted before scaling SEO.
- Avoid words like "guaranteed", "risk-free", "100% returns" in ads — they can trigger removal.
- Use **Risk Disclosure** prominently (already in footer).
- Avoid claims comparable to gambling/betting platforms — RBI now restricts such advertising in India.
